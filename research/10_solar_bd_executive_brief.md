# Prove the yield first, then land anchor IPPs

The evidence says a solar self-cleaning vendor sells **recovered kilowatt-hours and removed risk, not cleaning**, and that the one route that has produced every documented commercial breakthrough in this category is the same: a proof-first pilot with a sponsor who has a non-price reason to say yes, converted into a master supply agreement with one anchor developer, then replicated through EPC and O&M channels. Soiling costs the industry **4–7% of global PV output and multi-billion euros a year** ([IEA-PVPS fact sheet 2025](https://iea-pvps.org/fact-sheets/fs-soiling-losses/)), yet the global cleaning service line is only about **USD 1.2–1.5 billion** today (market-research firms, methodology opaque) and the buyers who matter most, India and the Gulf, have already standardised on cheap Chinese row robots at GW scale, so a new entrant competes against a **Sol-Bright/LEAPTING price benchmark**, not against manual labour ([Mercom India](https://www.mercomindia.com/top-robotic-solar-module-cleaning-equipment-suppliers-in-2025)). The decision-making unit is the asset manager (commercial, insurance compliance) plus the Head of O&M (performance ratio and the cleaning budget), gated by the module OEM's warranty, the insurer's documentation demands and the lender's independent engineer; their dominant fear is **module-surface damage**, so the first artefacts a seller needs are an IEC 62788-7-3 abrasion report and a module-OEM compatibility letter, not an ROI calculator. India is the largest live market (171 GW installed, 44.6 GW added in FY2025-26, a 208 GW pipeline, with Rajasthan and Gujarat holding ~79 GW of desert capacity), but it requires an Indian entity, ~27% landed duty on powered equipment, BIS marking from October 2026 and relationship-first selling. The lawful guerrilla tactics that work are data-led (a public soiling-loss index, data-only soiling audits, structured risk-reversal pilots, hosted dinners near REI, Intersolar India and the Solarplaza asset-management summits); unsanctioned drone imagery, uninvited site entry and cold WhatsApp or SMS are the tactics to exclude. Nearly every figure below comes from search-result excerpts rather than full documents, so each is labelled fact, vendor claim, practitioner opinion or market-research claim, and each section closes with what still needs verifying.

## Soiling costs 4–7% of output, but the cleaning budget is small and shrinking per megawatt

The headline a salesperson can quote with confidence is the IEA-PVPS Task 13/16 fact sheet of September 2025: soiling removes an average **4–7% of global PV energy**, a loss worth billions of euros a year even with optimised cleaning ([IEA-PVPS fact sheet](https://iea-pvps.org/fact-sheets/fs-soiling-losses/); [pv magazine, Oct 2025](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2025/10/01/iea-pvps-warns-soiling-costs-solar-industry-billions-per-year/)). The underlying peer-reviewed work, Ilse et al. in Joule, put the 2018 loss at 3–4% of global production and at least EUR 3–5 billion of revenue, projecting 4–7% and more than EUR 4–7 billion by 2023 ([Joule](https://www.cell.com/joule/fulltext/S2542-4351(19)30422-2)). Cite the year with every figure, because the 2022 IEA report said 3–5% and older NREL-attributed numbers say 5–10% ([IEA-PVPS 2022](https://iea-pvps.org/key-topics/soiling-losses-impact-on-the-performance-of-photovoltaic-power-plants/)). Regional rates are what make the business case: DEWA's desert test site beside the Mohammed bin Rashid solar park measured baseline soiling of **0.14–0.33% per day**, which daily robotic cleaning cut below 0.02% per day ([pv magazine, Aug 2026](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/08/14/dewa-study-assesses-pv-cleaning-robot-performance-and-safety/)); Saudi sites run 0.1–0.57% per day seasonally and Abu Dhabi reached 0.87% per day in its worst month ([ResearchGate, Saudi soiling 2022](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/364844999_The_Impact_of_Soiling_on_PV_Module_Performance_in_Saudi_Arabia)); the northern Atacama coast loses up to **39% of annual energy** ([ResearchGate, Atacama 2021](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/354663174_Quantifying_PV_soiling_losses_in_the_Atacama_Desert)). India's peer-reviewed range is 0.05–0.55% per day with measured daily averages of 0.17–0.21% and losses above 5% when cleaning intervals stretch ([Scientific Reports, 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-45485-2)); the 15–35%-after-a-month figures circulating for Rajasthan and Gujarat are vendor claims ([Heaven Green Energy, vendor](https://www.heavengreenenergy.com/blog/solar-cleaning-frequency-india)). Australia's farms lose 8–9% to dust that seasonal rain then removes, with persistent losses only around the Lake Eyre interior ([Applied Energy 2022](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S030626192200099X)), and the US Southwest has the highest modelled and measured soiling in NREL's 255-system fleet map ([NREL FY24](https://docs.nrel.gov/docs/fy24osti/88769.pdf); [OSTI](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1832410)). A simple rule follows: a single uncleaned month in the Gulf or northern Chile costs 3–25% of output, and these low-tilt desert sites are the economic core of any self-cleaning pitch, whereas bifacial rear-side soiling is roughly nine times lower than front-side and a second-order concern ([ResearchGate, bifacial](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/327363934_Effect_of_soiling_in_bifacial_PV_modules_and_cleaning_schedule_optimization)).

The money, however, is thinner than the loss. Wood Mackenzie counts **348 GW under third-party O&M** at end-2025, with the top 15 providers holding 57% and North American full-wrap O&M prices down **18% year on year** ([Wood Mackenzie](https://www.woodmac.com/ja/press-releases/global-solar-pv-om-market-reaches-348-gw-as-consolidation-tightens-and-costs-fall/)). LBNL's empirical median for US utility-scale O&M has fallen to about **USD 11/kWac-year** ([LBNL 2024](https://eta-publications.lbl.gov/sites/default/files/2024-10/utility_scale_solar_2024_edition_slides.pdf)) against NREL's modelled USD 22–24/kW-year ([NREL ATB 2024](https://atb.nrel.gov/electricity/2024/utility-scale_pv)). Dollar sizing of cleaning exists only from market-research houses, which cluster at USD 1.2–1.5 billion in 2024/25 growing to USD 2.3–2.5 billion by 2030/31 at 8–9% a year (Mordor: USD 1.41 billion in 2025 to USD 2.34 billion by 2031 ([Mordor, MR firm](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/solar-panel-cleaning-market)); Strategic Market Research: USD 1.2 billion to USD 2.5 billion ([Strategic MR, MR firm](https://www.strategicmarketresearch.com/market-report/solar-panel-cleaning-market))), while robot-only estimates diverge five-fold, from USD 161 million ([24MarketReports, MR firm](https://www.24marketreports.com/manufacturing-and-construction/global-photovoltaic-cleaning-robot-market)) to USD 1.2 billion ([openPR, MR firm](https://www.openpr.com/news/4474156/solar-panel-cleaning-robot-market-size-growth-forecast)) for 2024/25. Treat all of these as directional. The structural trend is more useful than the sizing: modules sit near USD 0.09/Wdc ([NREL, Spring 2025](https://docs.nrel.gov/docs/fy25osti/95135.pdf)) and practitioners now argue OPEX is where LCOE is won because "there are very limited places where we can still reduce capex cost" ([PV Tech](https://www.pv-tech.org/why-buying-cheap-is-costing-pv-developers-their-roi/)), which favours a "labour-free, water-free yield recovery" framing, though the exact OPEX share of LCOE is contested between 20–30% and a 2026 review's 77% ([Solar Energy review, 2026](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0038092X26007322)), so quote absolute LBNL and NREL numbers rather than a share.

### What owners actually pay, and why water is the Indian and Gulf lever

Per-pass pricing is vendor-reported and disagrees by an order of magnitude. A US price list quotes **USD 0.75–1.75 per panel at 10 MW falling to USD 0.37–0.75 per panel at 101–250 MW** ([Soilar Technologies, vendor](https://soilar.tech/chapter/chapter-3-the-solution/solar-panel-cleaning-pricing/)); in India, Mercom quotes manual cleaning at **INR 1–1.2 per module including water versus INR 0.20 robotic**, "six times cheaper" ([Mercom India](https://www.mercomindia.com/robotic-solar-module-cleaning-is-six-times-cheaper-than-traditional-means)), and Taypro's OPEX tariff runs INR 0.45–0.70 per module per cycle ([Taypro, vendor](https://taypro.in/solar-cleaning-opex-pricing)). Ecoppia has claimed paybacks of 1.5–6 years depending on geography and tariff ([Bridge to India interview, vendor](https://bridgetoindia.com/reviews-exclusive-interview-with-ecoppia/)) and that robots are only 1–2% of site capex ([Saur Energy, vendor](https://www.saurenergy.com/solar-energy-news/robotic-cleaning-high-performance-solar-panels-will-be-vital-to-indian-solar-market-with-tracking-mounting-sys-amit-singla-ecoppia-india)). Water is the binding constraint in the arid markets: manual washing uses up to 24,000 litres per MW per wash by one vendor's count ([Heaven Green, vendor](https://www.heavengreenenergy.com/blog/how-to-clean-solar-panels)), Bridge to India measured 1–4 litres per module per cycle ([Renewable Watch](https://renewablewatch.in/2020/12/03/waterless-way/)), and an Indian industry estimate puts annual use near **218 m3 per MW**, about 0.1 m3 per MWh generated ([SolarPost](https://solarpost.in/om/role-water-long-term-performance-solar-pv-plants/)). Express a water KPI in litres per MW per year; at twelve washes a year that is roughly 100–300 m3 per MW.

The KPIs the budget owner reports decide how the pitch must be framed. Guaranteed PR and guaranteed availability are the core contractual O&M KPIs ([IEA-PVPS O&M Guidelines 2022](https://iea-pvps.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/IEA-PVPS-Report-T13-25-2022-OandM-Guidelines.pdf)), but PR warranties are retreating from third-party O&M contracts ([SolarPower Europe Best Practices](https://solarbestpractices.com/guidelines/detail/contractual-framework)) and owners are moving to energy-based availability of 98% or more ([Greensolver, practitioner](https://greensolver.net/articles/key-solar-om-contract-elements-asset-owners-should-adopt-to-improve-their-overall-asset-performance/)). Availability does not capture soiling: "a plant can have 99% availability and 72% PR because it is always on but heavily soiled" ([Oxmaint, practitioner](https://oxmaint.com/industries/facility-management/solar-pv-system-maintenance-commercial-buildings)), and operators trigger cleaning when the soiling ratio drops below about 0.97 ([Blu Banyan](https://blubanyan.com/solar-asset-performance-monitoring-kpi-list/)). The implication is that the O&M contractor's liquidated-damages exposure does not pull cleaning technology; the owner's asset manager, who answers to lenders on DSCR and carries any PPA production guarantee, captures the soiling-recovery value and is the economic buyer. The bankability angle is sharper still: financial models typically carry a flat 1–3% soiling line ([IEA-PVPS T13-08, 2017](https://iea-pvps.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Report_IEA-PVPS_T13-08_2017_Technical_Assumptions_Used_in_PV_Financial_Models.pdf)), PVsyst defaults to 2% with 3–5% for conservative P95 cases ([Heaven Designs, practitioner](https://heavendesigns.in/blog/bankable-pvsyst-reports-guide/)), independent engineers flag unjustified soiling lines ([SurgePV, practitioner](https://www.surgepv.com/blog/solar-independent-engineer)), and kWh Analytics shows **US fleets running 8.6% below P50 in 2025** with optimistic P50s the largest driver ([kWh Analytics SRA 2025](https://kwhanalytics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Solar-Risk-Assessment-2025.pdf)), after its 2021 edition found IE soiling estimates badly off versus on-site measurement ([SRA 2021](https://kwhanalytics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/kWhAnalytics_SolarRiskAssessment21_06-08-21.pdf)). A documented reduction of the soiling line from 2–5% to about 1% lifts P50 and P90 directly, which is worth more to a developer than OPEX savings, but IEs will only accept it with measured data, so bundling DustIQ or MARS-class monitoring ([Kipp & Zonen DustIQ](https://www.kippzonen.com/Product/419/DustIQ-Soiling-Monitoring-System); [IEEE Qatar validation](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10749590/)) turns a marketing claim into bankable evidence.

Gaps to verify: no independent survey of utility-scale cleaning prices exists; no source quantified module breakage from cleaning; no published case of a lender or IE formally crediting a cleaning technology with a lower soiling assumption; cleaning frequency by region is only implied by vendor pages (weekly to monthly in India and the Gulf, one to four times a year in the US and Australia); the India-wide soiling figures beyond the Roorkee study are vendor numbers; and the IEA-PVPS 2022/2025 cost-of-cleaning sections could not be read.

## Chinese row robots set the price benchmark, and no pioneer won its first customer in a tender

Waterless autonomous row robots are the only self-cleaning category with multi-GW commercial scale, and leadership has shifted since 2023 from the Israeli pioneers to Chinese manufacturers and an Indian champion. Mercom's 2025 leaderboard gives **Langfang Sol-Bright ~35% of Indian robotic-cleaning shipments** (18% in 2024), with Solabot, LEAPTING, Aegeus and Vayu Solar completing a top five that shipped ~82% ([Mercom India](https://www.mercomindia.com/top-robotic-solar-module-cleaning-equipment-suppliers-in-2025)); Taypro had led in 2024 with 27.5% ([Mercom 2024](https://www.mercomindia.com/top-robotic-solar-module-cleaning-equipment-suppliers-in-2024)). LEAPTING alone claims **33,000 G1 robots across nine Indian states** (vendor claim) ([Solar Quarter, May 2026](https://solarquarter.com/2026/05/25/from-9-states-to-12-gw-inside-leapting-robotics-massive-indian-expansion-gaurav-gaba-sales-director-india/)) on the back of a 2 GW Adani agreement ([Mercom](https://www.mercomindia.com/leapting-to-supply-module-cleaning-robots-for-adanis-2-gw-solar-project)), 2,000-plus robots for Waaree's 1.2 GWp on 2P fixed tilt ([pv magazine India, Jan 2026](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2026/01/08/leapting-to-deploy-2000-g1-cleaning-robots-across-waaree-renewables-1-2-gwp-solar-projects/)), a >1 GW ReNew order on trackers ([Mercom](https://www.mercomindia.com/leapting-secures-1-gw-solar-module-cleaning-robot-order-from-renew)) and a BluPine deal signed on the REI Expo floor ([Energetica India](https://www.energetica-india.net/news/leapting-signs-agreement-with-blupine-energy-for-2000-solar-cleaning-robots-at-rei-expo-2026)). Sunpure raised a Series A above CNY 100 million ([EqualOcean](https://equalocean.com/news/2024013120480-sunpure-technology-secures-cny-100m-series-photovoltaic-power-plant-innovation)), put ~1,054 robots on ACWA Power's Red Sea project ([Solar Quarter](https://solarquarter.com/2024/01/15/red-sea-project-sunpure-is-featured-in-chinese-state-television-cctv/)) and sold a 473 MWp tracker project through EPC Jakson Green ([MESIA](https://mesia.com/sunpure-signs-strategic-robotic-cleaning-solution-contract-for-473-mwp-tracker-based-project-with-jakson-green-private-limited/)). The economics of the pioneers collapsed in parallel: Ecoppia raised USD 82.5 million in a 2020 Tel Aviv IPO at a NIS 1.3 billion valuation ([PV Tech](https://www.pv-tech.org/robotic-solar-cleaning-specialist-ecoppia-lands-us82-5m-in-ipo/)) and sold its business to its CEO for **NIS 18 million** in March 2024 ([Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1001473387)); BladeRanger "failed in marketing its robot" and its SolarDrone unit went to a Nasdaq shell ([Calcalist Tech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/vx47cgrv9)); Germany's Sun-X filed for insolvency in March 2026 ([Solarserver](https://www.solarserver.de/2026/03/02/solarmodulreinigung-sun-x-im-insolvenzverfahren/)). Demand is not the problem, margin is: Adani, Waaree, BluPine and ACWA order thousands of robots but source from the lowest-cost manufacturer, so the benchmark a new technology must beat is a low-cost Chinese row robot, not a premium Israeli one. The only strategic exit was a tracker OEM buying a bundled feature, Nextracker's 2025 purchase of Amir Robotics ([Nextracker 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1852131/000185213125000047/ex991_q126.htm)), which marks tracker and module OEMs as the most credible acquirers and partners.

Every non-robot category remains pilot-scale. DSM's (now Covestro's) factory-applied anti-soiling coating has the best field evidence, **+2.5% generation versus anti-reflective-only modules at a 150 MW Andhra Pradesh plant** ([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/349496401_25_Improvement_in_generation_utilizing_Anti_soiling_modules_based_on_DSM_technology)), and a nanoporous metal-oxide coating showed a 3.52% mean lifetime gain at 10-degree tilt in a peer-reviewed trial ([Solar Energy Materials and Solar Cells, 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0927024823003811)); Pellucere sells a field-applied AR+AS coating with vendor-claimed gains of 4–5% plus up to 4–8% in dusty sites ([PV Tech, vendor claims](https://www.pv-tech.org/pellucere-technologies-offers-worlds-first-field-installable-anti-reflectiv/)), while NREL's five-year multi-site study found dry-brush abrasion of a porous silica coating accelerates at roughly a factor of one, meaning lab brushing approximates field wear ([OSTI](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1458821)). Sol Clarity's electrodynamic screen, spun out of Boston University, ran a first commercial pilot in December 2023, holds letters of intent from Lightsource bp, Engie and Scatec, and raised a USD 920K seed with Equinor Ventures and Techstars ([USGlass](https://www.usglassmag.com/startup-pioneers-first-successful-field-trial-of-electrodynamic-screen-for-solar-glass/); [JPT](https://jpt.spe.org/twa/boston-university-alumni-develop-clean-solar-panel-technology)); MIT's electrostatic method has no spinout identified ([MIT MechE](https://meche.mit.edu/node/3298)); Fraunhofer IDMT showed structure-borne-sound cleaning at Intersolar 2025 ([pv magazine India](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2025/05/02/fraunhofer-presents-solar-module-cleaning-tech-using-structure-borne-sound)). No coating, EDS, vibration or drone vendor has a disclosed multi-hundred-MW reference fleet as of late 2026.

### How the winners landed their first customers

Across every case the first commercial site was in the vendor's home market with a sponsor who had a non-financial reason to say yes, and none came through a competitive tender. Ecoppia pilot-tested for about three years with Siemens, then part-owner of Arava Power ([TriplePundit, 2014](https://triplepundit.com/2014/cloud-based-solar-robotics-platform-cleans-pv-panels-without-water/)), then rolled nearly 100 E4 robots across the whole Ketura Sun field "in less than three months" after the pilot and marketed it as the world's first autonomously cleaned park (vendor framing) ([PR Newswire, 2014](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/ecoppia-announces-worlds-first-completely-autonomously-cleaned-solar-energy-park-251939791.html)); it added First Solar certification in 2016 ([EnergyTrend](https://www.energytrend.com/news/20160105-10011.html)), won Bhadla the same year ([pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2016/12/07/ecoppia-module-cleaning-solution-chosen-for-bhadla-solar-park-in-india_100027141)) and was used by Engie to bid a lower LCOE at Bhadla ([Israel Science Info](https://israelscienceinfo.com/en/engie-group-leverages-ecoppia-israel-to-deliver-lower-lcoe-higher-output-in-successful-indian-solar-park-bid/)). Airtouch's first customer "as far as is known" was Enlight in Israel ([Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1001359930)); it entered India with a USD 3 million ReNew deal ([pv magazine India, Mar 2023](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2023/03/02/airtouch-solar-signs-3-million-cleaning-robot-deal-with-renew/)), then a USD 2.5 million Adani order ([pv magazine India, May 2023](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2023/05/15/airtouch-solar-signs-2-5-million-dry-cleaning-robot-deal-with-adani-green-energy/)) that became a **master supply agreement with three AGEL orders in nine months** ([pv magazine India, Apr 2024](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2024/04/04/airtouch-solar-secures-6-million-cleaning-robot-contract-with-adani-green-energy/)), backed by a wholly owned subsidiary and Noida factory ([Energetica India interview](https://www.energetica-india.net/powerful-thoughts/online/nimish-jain-12-09-2023)). NOMADD was funded by KAUST's own USD 1 million "for marketing in Saudi Arabia, hiring staff, and testing with potential customers" ([PV Tech](https://www.pv-tech.org/saudi-arabian-solar-module-cleaning-technology-start-up-secures-us1-million/)), treated EPCs as "the main distributors in this sector" ([TechCrunch, 2016](https://techcrunch.com/2016/01/10/saudi-arabian-startup-nomadd-looks-to-clean-up-solar-energy-production/)), took an Aramco partnership ([PV Tech](https://pv-tech.org/saudi-aramco-and-start-up-partner-on-waterless-solar-cleaning-solutions)) and a construction-group investor for local manufacturing ([KAUST](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/en/news/kaust-startup-nomadd-secures-investment-from-cepco)), and reached NEOM through contractor SATCO ([KAUST](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/news/nomadd-sets-sights-on-solar-energy-future)). SolarCleano bootstrapped on Luxinnovation grants, delivered 20 robots in year one and exported to desert markets ([Forbes Luxembourg](https://www.forbes.lu/solarcleano-solar-farm-cleaning-robots/)); Taypro grew on about USD 67K of seed capital from an industrial group ([Tracxn, aggregator](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/taypro/__TSxXl8358rp0Oj57Zk0F1Gu_cB7FpIMeEoTxrPlARDQ)), reference-customer marketing (Tata Power, Avaada, Amplus, NLC, HPCL as vendor claims) and a pay-per-verified-panel OPEX model ([Taypro](https://taypro.in/solar-panel-cleaning-system/solar-panel-cleaning-service)). The Chinese entrants scaled by riding ACWA's gigaprojects and by local sales teams signing framework deals with Indian IPPs at trade expos. The pattern that recurs is pilot at one plant, master supply agreement with that IPP, repeat orders on its next projects, and specification of the equipment alongside the tracker or structure, which is why EPCs such as Waaree Renewable Technologies and AMP/AMPIN are now buyers as well as IPPs. Capex hardware sale under framework agreements is the dominant model; Taypro's per-verified-panel OPEX is the only documented cleaning-as-a-service structure, and no vendor publishes a per-MW-year RaaS price or carries yield risk.

Gaps to verify: no current unit prices for Chinese G1, Sunpure or Taypro robots (the 2019 Ecoppia benchmark of INR 10–20 lakh per unit is obsolete); no audited revenue for any private vendor and Airtouch's and Ecoppia's TASE filings were not read; PitchBook's listing of a StarkPower acquisition of Ecoppia in January 2026 is uncorroborated ([PitchBook, aggregator](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/100184-14)); "Skyline Robotics" and "Crystal Clear" returned nothing in solar cleaning and are unresearched; who personally championed the Bhadla, Adani or Enlight decisions could not be established.

## Asset managers buy risk removal first and recovered kWh second

The decision-making unit at an IPP or utility is wider than one executive, and the documented roles split cleanly. SolarPower Europe's asset-management guidelines make the **asset manager** "crucial for identifying, selecting, and properly managing key suppliers" ([Solar Best Practices: procurement](https://solarbestpractices.com/guidelines/detail/procurement-3)), give the O&M contractor "a more technical role (energy output optimisation)" while the asset manager handles "financial optimisation", and make the asset manager responsible for checking that site equipment meets insurance-policy requirements because non-compliance can void cover ([Solar Best Practices: asset management](https://solarbestpractices.com/guidelines/detail/asset-management)). Generic B2B evidence says a complex purchase involves six to ten decision-makers ([Brixon Group summarising Gartner](https://brixongroup.com/en/the-modern-b2b-buying-journey-why-buyers-complete-80-of-their-journey-alone-and-how-you-can-still-remain-visible)), buyers spend only about 17% of purchase time with vendors, and content tailored to the whole buying group lifts consensus 20% ([Madison Logic summarising Gartner](https://www.madisonlogic.com/blog/navigating-the-fall-of-the-individual-buyer-and-the-rise-of-the-buying-committee/)). Utility-sales practitioners add that the champion should sit at VP or Director level in the department that will use the product, that procurement "focuses on vendor viability" and adds four to eight weeks, and that security documentation should be ready before procurement starts ([Salesforce Ventures, practitioner opinion](https://salesforceventures.com/perspectives/utility-sales/)); that an engineer champion cannot sign alone, so multi-thread early ([Dragon Energy, practitioner](https://dragonenergy.substack.com/p/a-founders-guide-to-selling-technology)); and that "utilities prioritize reliability in virtually all purchases", innovation departments "can be an entry point but are not where you close", and products that do not fit an existing budget category stall ([The Impact, practitioner](https://readtheimpact.com/why-clean-energy-startups-underestimate-selling-to-utilities/); [Enduring Planet, practitioner](https://enduringplanet.com/insights/selling-to-utilities-as-a-climate-startup)). Mapped onto a self-cleaning sale (an inference from these role descriptions, not a single source), the champion is the Head or VP of O&M or Asset Management who owns PR and the cleaning budget, the technical gate is the plant O&M manager and incumbent O&M contractor, the commercial gate is procurement, and the external gates are the module OEM (warranty), the insurer (maintenance logs) and the lender's independent engineer at financial close or refinancing. Plan on "6 months to pilot, 18 months to contract" for a utility ([Dragon Energy, practitioner](https://dragonenergy.substack.com/p/a-founders-guide-to-selling-technology)).

The dominant, documented fear is module damage. PI Berlin found that "cleaning robots can cause long-term module damage" ([PV Tech](https://www.pv-tech.org/cleaning-robots-can-cause-long-term-module-damage-finds-pi-berlin/)), and its Juliane Berghold reports that robot use in desert regions "has led tender bidders to present independent confirmation of surface cleaning resistance" ([pv magazine press release](https://www.pv-magazine.com/press-releases/photovoltaik-institut-berlin-verifies-impact-of-cleaning-robots_100022221/)); PI Berlin's standardised procedure runs an accelerated **3,100 cleaning cycles with sand on float glass** (figure from a garbled abstract, verify) ([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/336718524_STANDARDIZED_TEST_PROCEDURE_FOR_PV_MODULE_CLEANING_EQUIPMENT)). IEC 62788-7-3:2022, amended 2024, now standardises abrasion tests with linear brush, rotary brush, falling sand and sand impingement methods ([NREL](https://research-hub.nrel.gov/en/publications/the-iec-62788-7-3-abrasion-methods-standard-and-supporting-experi/); [PV Tech](https://www.pv-tech.org/pv-cleaning-robots-new-test-methods-for-a-new-technology/)), and QEERI's field work found abrasion is module-specific: "five kinds of module showed greater losses with more frequent cleaning, while the other four did not" ([HBKU/QEERI](https://elmi.hbku.edu.qa/en/publications/real-world-performance-and-module-degradation-impacts-of-robotic-/)). Vendors already compete on this: Ecoppia claims PI Berlin certification for nine module brands simulating 20 years of daily cleaning (vendor claim) ([Ecoppia](https://www.ecoppia.com/media/press/?ContentID=38042)), Solaris Hydrobotics claims written non-voiding letters from Trina and GCL (vendor claim) ([Solaris Hydrobotics](https://solarishydrobotics.com/)), and Taypro says insurers "now demand granular documentation of maintenance logs" when shifting to automated cleaning (vendor claim) ([Taypro](https://taypro.in/blog/insurance-considerations-for-robotic-solar-cleaning-assets)). Module makers set the rules in their manuals: First Solar requires cleaning systems to pass its approval process and excludes modules "deemed damaged by cleaning methods" ([First Solar cleaning guidelines, snippet only](https://www.firstsolar.com/-/media/First-Solar/Technical-Documents/Series-4-Application-Note/Module-Cleaning-Guidelines.pdf)), and Jinko's manual says "never use chemicals when cleaning modules as this may affect the module warranty" ([Jinko installation manual 2024](https://www.jinkosolar.com/uploads/JinkoSolar%20Global%20Installation%20Manual_20240814_A1.pdf)). Tracker OEMs have become a second gate: Nextracker issued a compatibility letter to KP Group's robot ([UNI India](https://test.uniindia.com/kp-group-39-s-solar-panel-cleaning-robot-receives-approval-from-nextracker/pnn/news/3434276.html)) and GameChange markets its Genius Tracker as integrating with "leading solar module robotic cleaning solutions" ([GameChange Solar](https://www.gamechangesolar.com/news/gamechange-solar-announces-seamless-integration-of-genius-tracker-with-robotic-cleaners)). Bankability for cleaning technology therefore decomposes into four artefacts that no single source lists but the gates imply: an IEC 62788-7-3-based abrasion report on the buyer's specific glass and coating, written module-OEM non-voiding letters for the modules on the target site, a maintenance-log data trail an insurer accepts, and an IE-reviewable soiling-loss and gain model with a measured control. For a coating the hard question is whether it survives the robot fleet the plant already runs, since NREL found dry-brush field wear tracks lab brushing ([OSTI](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1458821)).

### Buying triggers, pilot structures that convert, and the programmes that host them

Four documented triggers open the budget. O&M contracts run 5–15 years at utility scale ([Heaven Green glossary, practitioner](https://www.heavengreenenergy.com/glossary/o-and-m-contract-types)), most C&I customers "sign a 5-year initial O&M contract at commissioning, then renew or renegotiate" ([Independent Solar, practitioner](https://independentsolar.com/commercial-solar-operations-and-maintenance-contracts-guide-2026/)), and owners are advised to benchmark or retender every three to five years ([PV-Maps, practitioner](https://pv-maps.com/en/blog/solar-om-costs-per-mw)), so the 12–18 months before a term expiry is the first window. The second is measured underperformance: the first annual review after COD, when the 8.6%-below-P50 gap becomes visible ([kWh Analytics SRA 2025](https://kwhanalytics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Solar-Risk-Assessment-2025.pdf)). The third is water: SolarPower Europe's O&M guidelines state that "in areas with water scarcity, water use for module cleaning should be minimised" ([SPE O&M Guidelines v5.0 mirror](https://nomadelectric.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/OM-best-practice-guidelines.pdf); [v6.0 release, Feb 2025](https://www.solarpowereurope.org/press-releases/solar-power-europe-launches-new-operation-and-maintenance-best-practices-report-2)), and MNRE's 2019 letter asked Indian developers to adopt robotic cleaning and anti-soiling coatings to conserve water, an advisory rather than a mandate ([Mercom India](https://www.mercomindia.com/mnre-asks-robotics-for-water-solar)). The fourth is contractual: Mordor claims stricter performance-ratio O&M contracts push soiling risk onto service providers and that "cleaning cadence, not equipment price" decides awards (market-research claim) ([Mordor, MR firm](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/solar-panel-cleaning-market)), which would make the O&M contractor a buyer in its own right rather than only a blocker. Budget classification matters: O&M is planned at roughly 1–2% of capex a year ([ElectricBird, practitioner](https://electricbird.vn/blogs/solar-om-costs-process-budget/)) and accounts for about 77% of utility-scale OPEX ([Solar Energy review 2026](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0038092X26007322)), so a per-clean or per-MWh service fits an existing opex line while an owned fleet competes with scarce capex and may need IE review at refinancing. In the Gulf a foreign vendor also hits local-content gates: Saudi IKTVA ratios "will be used to determine the award of contracts" ([Aramco IKTVA guideline](https://iktva.sa/Library/Assets/Gallery/Downloads/Guidelines/Iktva%20Doing%20business%20with%20SA%202018.pdf)) and the UAE ICV score does not transfer across borders ([Project 54](https://projectfifty4.com/iktva-icv-local-content-gcc/)).

Documented solar pilots are small and host-funded. Enel Green Power tested the SandStorm robot at its Passo Martino lab and then on a **1 MW section of its Totana plant** ([Enel Green Power](https://www.enelgreenpower.com/stories/articles/2018/10/solar-panel-cleaning-egp-innovative-solution)); Iberdrola's PERSEO "NewTech Challenge: Photovoltaic cleaning" drew more than 70 proposals from 25 countries, picked four winners (MoreSun coatings, SCM and SolarCleano robots, Kipp & Zonen soiling sensors), bore the pilot costs at Núñez de Balboa and offered "official global supplier" status with possible PERSEO investment ([Iberdrola challenge](https://www.iberdrola.com/innovation/international-startup-program-perseo/startup-challenge-photovoltaic-cleaning); [Power Engineering International](https://www.powerengineeringint.com/renewables/solar/start-ups-solve-solar-innovation-challenge-set-by-iberdrola/)); Enel's 2023 anti-soiling coatings challenge judged "mainly by the extra energy a plant produces" ([InnoCentive](https://www.innocentive.com/challenges/anti-soiling-coatings-for-solar-panels/)). The best conversion benchmark is EDP's Free Electrons: **more than 60 pilots for about EUR 2 million produced more than 10 commercial contracts worth over EUR 35 million**, roughly one pilot in six ([ESB on Free Electrons](https://esb.ie/news---insights/blog/article/esb/2025/01/14/free-electrons--unlocking-the-power-of-global-energy-innovation)); the 2026 cycle (CLP, EDP, E.ON, ESB, Hydro Québec, Origin) ran applications 17 Oct 2025 to 31 Jan 2026 ([Free Electrons](https://freeelectrons.org/2026-program/)). Other open doors: Shell's New Energy Challenge (January deadline, feeds GameChanger and Shell Ventures) ([Shell](https://www.shell.com/what-we-do/technology-and-innovation/innovate-with-shell/shell-ventures/new-energy-challenge.html)), Shell GameChanger Accelerator powered by NREL with up to USD 250,000 of non-dilutive validation, referral-based ([SuperScout GCxN](https://superscout.co/accelerator/gcxn)), ACWA Power's Innovation Days, which produced seven partnership agreements in 2025 and sit on an open-innovation programme with 968 ideas and 59 projects ([ACWA Power IAR 2025](https://iar2025.acwapower.com/en/strategic-review/innovation-digital-review)), Third Derivative's 18-month hard-tech cohort ([RMI](https://rmi.org/news/third-derivative-unveils-26-startup-climate-cohort-focused-on-hard-tech-and-deployment/)), Elemental Impact's rolling 2026 intake ([Causo guide](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/how-to-apply-to-elemental-impact-2026)), NTPC's Startup Grand Challenge ([India Investment Grid](https://indiainvestmentgrid.gov.in/events/ntpc-startup-grand-challenge)), MNRE's INR 2.3 crore challenge with NISE, India-only and closed August 2025 ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/06/govt-launches-rs-23-cr-startup-programme-to-fast-track-rooftop-solar-installation)), and Tata Power's Pilot Accelerator, which offers techno-commercial pilot support up to INR 25 lakh to Social Alpha challenge winners ([Autocar Pro / Social Alpha](https://www.autocarpro.in/news-national/tata-trustsbacked-social-alpha-announces-winners-of-innovations-in-clean-energy-challenge-79531?amp=1)). Cross-industry practitioner literature converges on what makes a pilot convert: a named economic owner and written success criteria before launch ([Caymont, practitioner](https://insights.caymont.com/p/the-pilot-paradox)), "a commitment to a commercial contract if all KPIs were hit" ([Drew Kriens, practitioner](https://drewkriens.substack.com/p/escaping-pilot-purgatory)), a fixed window and pre-agreed price ([Technology Transfer Platform, practitioner](https://technologytp.com/en/the-ultimate-guide-to-startup-pilot-projects/)), the P&L owner in the room because innovation teams "rarely own the plant P&L", and treating an extension request at the go/no-go as "the first step into purgatory" ([Traction Technology, practitioner](https://www.tractiontechnology.com/blog/how-to-run-a-successful-pilot-with-a-startup-frameworks-kpis-enterprise-best-practices)). Utility pilots otherwise "never land" while startups run out of cash ([Latitude Media](https://www.latitudemedia.com/news/pilot-purgatory-the-quintessential-definition-of-insanity/)); Aquacycl's PepsiCo pilot took about 22 months to convert ([Cleantech Innovators](https://cleantechinnovators.substack.com/p/aquacycl-protocol-escaping-valley-of-death)). For a self-cleaning product the accepted metric is Enel's: incremental kWh against a control block, so insist on **alternating treated and control rows**, irradiance-corrected PR, independent soiling sensing and a run spanning a full dry-season soiling cycle ([Cotech Solar, practitioner](https://cotechsolar.com/guides/do-solar-cleaning-robots-pay-off/)), since one operator measured a >15% post-cleaning gain that faded within about two months ([ETMAX, practitioner](https://etmaxdoo.com/operating/cleaning/)), and net out robot-pass shading of ~0.16% of a day's yield in any robot comparison ([pv magazine India / QEERI](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2023/07/14/assessing-shading-effect-of-cleaning-robots-in-solar-farms/)). The most likely conversion killers for this product specifically are an incumbent O&M contractor whose cleaning scope is displaced without being made a beneficiary, a pilot that proves "it works" rather than incremental kWh at an agreed price, and warranty or insurance gates left uncleared so the asset manager cannot approve scale-up even when the champion wants to.

Gaps to verify: no source maps sign-off thresholds for O&M technology at IPPs; no module-OEM warranty letters for named robots from LONGi, Trina, JA or First Solar were found; no IE firm (DNV, Black & Veatch, UL, TÜV) statement on how unproven O&M technology is treated; no insurer has publicly endorsed or rejected automated cleaning; paid-pilot price points, data-sharing terms and the outcomes of Iberdrola's 2019 pilots were not found; no programme was found for Masdar, Lightsource bp, NextEra, Brookfield, Sembcorp, Adani, ReNew, JSW or Avaada, which should be read as unverified rather than absent; and all cited challenge deadlines have passed as of November 2026.

Execution at events is cheaper than it looks: attend-only at free shows costs travel; a 9–12 m2 Intersolar stand is roughly EUR 3,000–4,500 of space before build; a 10x10 ft RE+ booth is about USD 4,200–5,200 of space (inferred from cited rates); and at the mega-shows the yield comes from pre-booking 20-minute meetings two to four months ahead using the organiser's free exhibitor directory and speaker lists, then working the O&M and asset-management conference tracks. Speaking slots are an open route through The smarter E call for abstracts ([PES](https://pes.eu.com/press-releases/call-for-abstracts-now-open-contributions-wanted-for-the-smarter-e-europe-2026-conferences)).

Trade associations buy standing rather than meetings. SEIA Basic membership costs USD 900 (2026 brochure), with Kilowatt at USD 16,000 and event discounts starting at Watt level ([SEIA 2026 benefits PDF](https://seia.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/How-SEIA-Benefits-Your-Business-2026.pdf); [Kilowatt pricing](https://seia.org/sites/default/files/3.%202024%20SEIA%20Kilowatt%20Member%20Benefits%20-%20With%20Pricing%20-%20May%202024.pdf)), and SEIA has launched an O&M working group ([PV Tech](https://www.pv-tech.org/seia-launches-om-working-group-to-better-serve-all-sectors-of-the-industry)). SolarPower Europe membership gives access to all workstreams including the O&M Task Force that writes the annual O&M Best Practice Guidelines and runs the self-certified O&M Best Practices Mark ([SPE benefits](https://www.solarpowereurope.org/membership/membership-benefits); [pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/press-releases/solarpower-europe-launches-worlds-first-solar-om-best-practices-mark)), though its fee is indexed and unpublished. MESIA lists a package at AED 9,200 for 12 months on a development page (tier unclear) and publishes its member list ([MESIA packages](https://dev.mesia.com/membership-packages); [MESIA members](https://mesia.com/members/)); Australia's Clean Energy Council runs tiers from a small-company "Network" level to A$99,000 ([CEC apply](https://cleanenergycouncil.org.au/membership/apply-for-membership)); NSEFI in India has size-based tiers (secondary, low-reliability source) ([QuickEstimate](https://quickestimate.co/glossary/nsefi)).

Free identification sources substitute for paid databases. India's Bureau of Indian Standards publishes the composition of its ETD 28 solar working groups, naming member organisations and representatives ([BIS ETD 28 WG03](https://services.bis.gov.in/php/BIS_2.0/bisconnect/dgdashboard/committee_sso/composition/1306/5)); Solarplaza keeps a public database of organisations that attended its summits ([Solarplaza organisation page](https://www.solarplaza.com/organization/999/sonnedix/)); trade press names buyers and suppliers in every cleaning deal, Mercom's annual robotic-cleaning leaderboard being the richest single page ([Mercom](https://mercomindia.com/daily-news-wrap-up-leading-robotic-solar-module-cleaners-in-2025)), and Renewable Watch runs a dedicated tag for cleaning solutions in solar O&M ([Renewable Watch](https://renewablewatch.in/category/tech-focus/cleaning-solutions-in-solar-om-tech-focus/)); podcast guest pages (SunCast, Solar Maverick) and award finalist lists publish names and titles ([SunCast](https://podcasts.apple.com/ca/podcast/id1045871373); [HASI / Solar Maverick](https://www.hasi.com/news/susan-nickey-joins-the-solar-maverick-podcast)). Company registers expose directors rather than O&M heads, so pair them with LinkedIn and speaker lists. Paid tooling is modest: one Sales Navigator Core seat at about USD 90–120 a month ([JoinValley](https://www.joinvalley.co/blog/linkedin-sales-navigator-cost-explained-2026)), Apollo at USD 49–149 per seat ([Crustdata](https://crustdata.com/blog/zoominfo-vs-apollo)) and Hunter's free 50-credit plan ([CostBench](https://costbench.com/software/sales-intelligence/hunter-io/free-plan/)) cover a sub-10-person team; ZoomInfo's ~USD 33,500 median annual spend is over-scoped ([Crustdata](https://crustdata.com/blog/zoominfo-vs-apollo)).

Public tenders are a slow but reference-building route. NTPC issues solar O&M tenders through GeM, for example biennial O&M of a 25 MWp floating plant at Simhadri ([PSU Connect](https://www.psuconnect.in/psu-news/ntpc-issues-gem-tender-for-25-mw-floating-solar-o-m-at-simhadri)), and an earlier NTPC O&M tender in Madhya Pradesh recommended robotic cleaning for its lower water use ([Mercom](https://mercomindia.com/ntpc-floats-om-tender-solar-project-madhya-pradesh)); oil PSUs tender robotic cleaning directly, HPCL in January 2024 and IndianOil Petronas at Haldia in January 2026 ([Solar Quarter: HPCL](https://solarquarter.com/2024/01/12/hpcl-releases-tender-for-procurement-of-robotic-solar-panel-cleaning-system/); [Solar Quarter: IndianOil Petronas](https://solarquarter.com/2026/01/08/indianoil-petronas-invites-bids-for-robotic-solar-panel-cleaning-system-at-haldia-terminal)); SECI auctions multi-year O&M ([Solar Quarter](https://solarquarter.com/2025/11/08/almighty-green-energy-wins-secis-%e2%82%b94-12-crore-contract-for-five-year-om-of-10-mw-solar-project-in-rajasthan/)). In the Gulf, DEWA registers foreign suppliers through its SRM portal ([DEWA procurement](https://dewa.gov.ae/en/supplier/main-services/procurement)), but the real O&M buyers of the giga-plants are the IPP consortia, Masdar (1.8 GW MBR phase six, a 30 GW UAE agreement with EWEC) and ACWA Power ([PV Tech](https://www.pv-tech.org/masdar-ewec-sign-agreement-on-30gw-solar-pv-and-8gw-storage-deployment-in-uae/); [Proximo](https://www.proximoinfra.com/news/30944/DEWA-confirms-ACWA-consortium-for-900MW-solar-project)), and ACWA already specifies "an automated robotic cleaning solution" at design stage for Sudair ([ACWA Power](https://acwapower.com/en/what-we-do/projects/sudair-pv-ipp/)).

Warm-introduction routes carry the most weight. EPC and O&M contractors are where the large Indian orders flowed: Sol-Bright reached NTPC's 250 MW Bikaner project through EPC Rays Power ([Mercom](https://www.mercomindia.com/sol-bright-signs-deal-with-rays-power-for-solar-module-cleaning-robots)) and entered via O&M firm Solidus ([The Machine Maker](https://themachinemaker.com/news/solidus-techno-power-partners-with-sol-bright-to-expand-automated-om-capabilities/)); independent engineers are a credibility multiplier rather than a channel, because an owner's engineer may accept an aggressive soiling assumption while a lender's engineer will challenge it ("zero percent soiling is a myth") ([SurgePV](https://www.surgepv.com/blog/solar-lender-engineer)); and utility innovation hubs state the goal outright, "the startup becomes an Enel supplier" ([StartupsReal](https://startupsreal.com/?p=7738)).

Direct outreach is lawful everywhere but on different terms. In the US, CAN-SPAM "makes no exception for business-to-business email" and requires honest headers, a postal address and opt-out handling within ten business days ([Indium](https://www.indium.com/blog/can-spam-b2b-marcom); [TruVisibility](https://www.truvisibility.com/blog/can-you-send-marketing-emails-to-companies/)). In the EU and UK, legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) with a documented balancing test supports B2B email, but national ePrivacy rules can require opt-in and sole traders count as individuals ([Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/gdpr-cold-email-b2b); [iubenda](https://www.iubenda.com/en/help/22784-how-does-gdpr-affect-b2b)). In India, the DPDP Rules were notified in November 2025 with full compliance by **13 May 2027** ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1713500/regulatory-update-notification-of-the-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025)), the Act has no legitimate-interest basis so marketing will generally need consent and a named work email counts as personal data (vendor legal guides, confirm with counsel) ([Ken.so](https://ken.so/cold-email-laws/dpdp-india)), and TRAI's February 2025 TCCCPR amendment abolished inferred consent, treats mixed messages as promotional and fines unregistered senders up to INR 50,000 per violation ([TRAI press release](https://cms.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/PR_No.11of2025.pdf); [Khaitan & Co](https://khaitanco.com/thought-leadership/Efforts-to-Curb-Spam-Continue-TRAI-Releases-Second-Amendment-to-the-TCCCPR-2018); [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/unsolicited-commercial-communication/)), so use email, LinkedIn and referrals rather than SMS or calls there. Saudi Arabia requires opt-in consent for direct marketing by email, phone, SMS and WhatsApp ([Baker McKenzie, KSA](https://resourcehub.bakermckenzie.com/en/resources/global-data-and-cyber-handbook/emea/saudi-arabia/topics/cookies-online-tracking-and-direct-marketing)); the UAE position on B2B is contested and free zones have their own law ([Baker McKenzie, UAE](https://resourcehub.bakermckenzie.com/en/resources/global-data-and-cyber-handbook/emea/uae/topics/cookies-online-tracking-and-direct-marketing)).

Gaps to verify: Solarplaza ticket and sponsorship prices, SPE's fee schedule and NSEFI's fees could not be fetched; no SAPVIA, ISA, Cleantech Open, Engie New Ventures, ReNew, IIT incubator or Masdar accelerator routes surfaced; ACWA and Masdar supplier-registration portals were not checked; Solar Asset Management Asia dates were not found; FTC's own CAN-SPAM penalty figure and the DPDP treatment of business-contact data were not retrieved; and no source evidenced which prospecting tools solar sellers actually use.

## India: 171 GW, two desert states, and buyers who already own 30,000 robots

India is the largest live market for soiling solutions and the one where the price benchmark is set. MNRE data put cumulative solar at **171.05 GW on 30 September 2026**, of which 125.65 GW is ground-mounted, with 35.24 GW added in January–September 2026 ([Sustainable Construction Now citing MNRE](https://www.sustainableconstruction-now.com/article/556391/indias-solar-pv-capacity-crosses-171-gw-in-september-2026-as-installations-accelerate-nationwide)); FY2025-26 was a record **44.61 GW** ([pv magazine India, Apr 2026](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2026/04/10/india-installs-record-45-gw-solar-capacity-in-fy2026/)); and Mercom's large-scale pipeline stood at **208 GW** at end-June 2026 ([Mercom India](https://www.mercomindia.com/indias-large-scale-solar-pipeline-tops-200-gw)). Rajasthan (~44.4 GW) and Gujarat (~34.9 GW) lead the states ([Heaven Green citing MNRE](https://www.heavengreenenergy.com/blog/top-solar-energy-producing-states-in-india)), and both are arid, so the addressable utility-scale market is concentrated in a handful of districts: Jodhpur, Jaisalmer, Bikaner and Barmer in Rajasthan, Kutch and Banaskantha in Gujarat, plus Pavagada in Karnataka. Adani Green reports 9.4 GW operational at Khavda on 31 March 2026 on the way to 30 GW by 2029 ([AGEL Khavda report](https://www.adanigreenenergy.com/digital-reports/green-energy/2026/khavda-re-project.html)), where "frequent sandstorms necessitate cleaning of solar panels several times in a day" and AGEL "has committed to deploy waterless cleaning robots for the entire solar capacity" ([AGEL release, Mar 2024](https://www.adanigreenenergy.com/newsroom/media-releases/adani-green-energy-operationalizes-1000-mw-of-the-30000-mw-khavda-renewable-energy-park)). A field study at Bhadla found developers drawing cleaning water from canals, tubewells and ponds in an area of "acute water scarcity" at 7–8 litres per panel wash ([CENFA, 2024](https://www.cenfa.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Report_Solar-Power-in-India-A-Case-Study-of-the-Bhadla-Solar-Power-Park.pdf)), which makes "waterless" a social-licence argument with large IPPs, not only a cost one.

The cost ceiling is low. Indian utility O&M averages about **INR 3.5 lakh per MW per year**, "the majority" of it module cleaning, manpower and security, with semi-automatic robotic cleaning at ~INR 1.5 lakh and automatic at ~INR 1.2 lakh per MW-DC per year (industry article, roughly three years old) ([Saur Energy](https://www.saurenergy.com/solar-energy-articles/indias-solar-om-market-a-tale-of-increased-domination-of-new-evolving-technologies)); a glossary puts cleaning labour and water at INR 1–2 lakh per MW per year inside INR 4–8 lakh of comprehensive O&M ([Heaven Green, practitioner](https://www.heavengreenenergy.com/glossary/o-and-m)); Renewable Watch cites manual cleaning "up to Rs 750,000 per MW per year" ([Renewable Watch, 2023](https://renewablewatch.in/2023/10/01/efficiency-gains-solutions-for-solar-panel-maintenance/)); Taypro's robot lists at INR 3,00,000 a unit on B2B marketplaces ([TradeIndia](https://www.tradeindia.com/products/25-kg-taypro-aluminium-solar-panel-automatic-cleaning-robot-7791456.html)) and its OPEX tariff is INR 800–1,500 per MW per cycle (vendor) ([Taypro](https://taypro.in/insights/solar-cleaning-research-june-2026)). Taking 0.3% per day as a dry-season rate, about 2,000 kWh/kWp and tariffs near INR 2.5/kWh, each 1% of soiling costs roughly INR 50,000 per MW a year (researcher's arithmetic), so the INR 1–2 lakh of cleaning spend plus residual loss is the ceiling a coating or passive product must undercut, and it will be benchmarked against Sol-Bright and LEAPTING quotes, not against Western manual labour.

### Who decides, what it takes to be allowed to sell, and how to behave

Operations at the large IPPs sit with SVP or EVP-level heads reporting to a COO or CEO. ACME Solar's Ashutosh Singh is Senior Vice President and Head – O&M for the entire operating portfolio ([Renewable Watch, Feb 2026](https://renewablewatch.in/2026/02/17/ashutosh-singh-senior-vice-president-and-head-om-and-project-director-acme-solar)); Juniper Green's Deepak Katyal is EVP Projects Solar ([IndiaInfoline](https://www.indiainfoline.com/company/juniper-green-energy-ltd/reports/directors-report)); Adani Green centralises O&M in its Energy Network Operations Centre, which MD Vneet Jaain "was instrumental in setting up", with an O&M team that grew from 630 to about 1,100 people ([AGEL ENOC deck](https://www.adanigreenenergy.com/-/media/Project/GreenEnergy/Investor-Downloads/Analyst-Meet-Dynamic/February-2021---O-and-M-Excellence-through-ENOC/OM-Excellence-through-ENOC.pdf); [AGEL May 2023 presentation](https://www.adanigreenenergy.com/-/media/Project/GreenEnergy/Investor-Downloads/Equity-Investor-Presentations/May-2023.pdf)). The practical map (inference) is Head of O&M or asset management as technical gatekeeper, EVP Projects or Engineering as specification gatekeeper for new builds, Head of Procurement as commercial gate, COO or CEO as sponsor for anything new, and the promoter's office for multi-year MSAs at Adani, Avaada, ACME, Juniper and Hero. Expect to be asked for a free or heavily discounted pilot on one 10–50 MW block across a dry season, measured by the IPP's own analytics team against a control block, then a reference call with another IPP before any MSA (practitioner inference consistent with the Airtouch and Miraikikai entries) ([pv magazine, Miraikikai Bhadla trial](https://www.pv-magazine.com/press-releases/miraikikai-to-commence-dry-cleaning-robot-operation-at-bhadla-solar-park-rajasthan/)). Tata Power's October 2026 pilot of Ocean Sun's membrane floating solar at Mulshi, signed at the India–EFTA summit to evaluate "performance, reliability, cost competitiveness and scalability", is the template for how an Indian major trials foreign technology ([SolarQuarter](https://solarquarter.com/2026/10/08/tata-power-partners-with-norways-ocean-sun-to-pilot-300-kwp-membrane-based-floating-solar-technology-in-maharashtra/)).

Compliance pushes any foreign vendor toward an Indian entity. Powered cleaning robots classified under HS 8479.50 carry 7.5% basic duty plus a 10% surcharge on it and 18% IGST, roughly **27–28.5% landed** ([Seair](https://www.seair.co.in/custom-duty/hs-code-84795000.aspx)), a lighter line than modules per a pv magazine India commentary that also flags a BIS quality-control order from 1 October 2026 covering battery-operated cleaning equipment ([pv magazine India, Jul 2026](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2026/07/28/why-indias-renewable-energy-equipment-imports-are-getting-harder-not-easier/)); that order, DPIIT S.O. 1739(E) of 6 April 2026, requires BIS certification and the ISI mark for more than 90 appliance categories under IS 302 (Part 1) from 1 October 2026 for larger enterprises ([India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/india-electrical-appliances-qco-2026-bis-certification-guide-for-foreign-manufacturers-44541.html)); a chemical coating would not be an electrical appliance under it, though its own standards questions were not researched. ALMM governs modules and, from 1 June 2026, cells, and no source places cleaning equipment within it ([TaiyangNews](https://taiyangnews.info/markets/india-enforces-almm-list-ii-for-solar-cells)). To sell to PSUs, GeM requires an Indian presence (subsidiary, JV or authorised distributor with "Deemed OEM" status) ([Bizfoc](https://bizfoc.com/oem-panel-foreign-companies-gem)), and NTPC tenders restrict bids to Class-I local suppliers ([Mercom](https://www.mercomindia.com/ntpc-floats-tender-for-om-of-4-mw-solar-projects-in-noida)). DPIIT Startup India recognition excludes foreign subsidiaries ([Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/faqs-dpiit-recognition-for-start-ups/)). The MSMED Act caps payment to MSMEs at 45 days whatever the contract says ([The Jackrabbit](https://thejackrabbit.in/msme-delayed-payment-india-2026/)), but a survey of 2,800 businesses found **52% of B2B payments overdue beyond 90 days** in four major cities ([IBS Intelligence](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/indian-cities-report-52-of-b2b-payments-overdue-for-90-days/)). Entry structures are a subsidiary for sustained commercial activity, a liaison office that cannot earn income, a branch for time-limited projects, or a distributor with permanent-establishment risk if it becomes a de facto India office ([India Briefing](https://india-briefing.com/doing-business-guide/india/company-establishment/representative-office-branch-office-project-office-vs-llp-s-and-subsidiary-in-india)); every documented foreign entrant (Airtouch Noida, Ecoppia Punjab, LEAPTING's plans) chose a subsidiary with local assembly ([Renewables Now](https://renewablesnow.com/news/chinas-leapting-plans-to-make-solar-panel-cleaning-robots-in-india-1277152/)). For a coating or passive product the lowest-friction route is licensing or co-marketing with one of the five O&M providers that contracted ~86% of India's 2025 O&M capacity, Inox Green, Mitarsh Energy (self-reported 28 GWp under O&M), Jakson Green (5 GWp, and the O&M contractor for NTPC's 260 MW Bhadla plant), Inspire Clean Energy and Sterling & Wilson ([Mercom](https://www.mercomindia.com/daily-news-wrap-up-leading-solar-om-service-providers-in-2025); [Mitarsh](https://www.mitarsh.com/); [Powerline](https://powerline.net.in/2025/06/11/jakson-green-secures-1-gwp-solar-om-contracts/); [SolarQuarter](https://solarquarter.com/2022/05/02/jakson-wins-om-order-for-ntpcs-260-mw-bhadla-solar-power-plant/)); for a mechanical product, licensing or contract manufacturing with Taypro, Aegeus or Solabot is the alternative to a subsidiary.

Culturally the verified guidance is consistent: "decisions are usually left to higher-ranking individuals" and relationships precede transactions ([CIBT Visas](https://cibtvisas.com/blog/business-etiquette-india)), ignoring hierarchy "may hinder progress" ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/corporate-governance/683732/cultural-considerations-when-doing-business-in-india)), WhatsApp has 535 million-plus Indian users and 15 million business accounts ([AiSensy](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/whatsapp-statistics-for-businesses/)) and is the day-to-day channel once a relationship exists, while enterprise buyers default to email for formal steps ([Rework](https://resources.rework.com/insights/conversational-growth/whatsapp-in-b2b-sales-motion); [Vedain](https://vedain.com/blog/how-to-use-whatsapp-for-b2b-sales-in-india-a-complete-guide)). The calendar guidance is practitioner inference, not sourced: avoid the Diwali fortnight and the last two weeks of March (fiscal year-end), expect January–February budget setting, and use REI (autumn, Greater Noida), Bharat Renewable Expo (January, Jaipur) and Intersolar India (March, Gandhinagar) as anchor weeks for hosted dinners and site-visit tours; the Jaipur show is likely the higher-yield January choice because India Energy Week in Kolkata the same week is ministerial and oil-and-gas heavy.

Gaps to verify: MNRE's primary monthly reports and the Make-in-India local-content thresholds (Class-I at 50% or more) were not read; no independent 2025-26 benchmark for manual cleaning labour cost or cleaner wages; no CGWA or Rajasthan groundwater order restricting panel washing was found; no source names the current O&M heads at ReNew, Adani Green, Tata Power Renewable, NTPC Renewable, Avaada, Hero, Azure, Sembcorp or O2 Power; whether AGEL's Khavda waterless commitment is fully executed, and by which vendors, is unconfirmed; GST on cleaning services and the HS code for a coating were not researched; Diwali and fiscal-year timing have no documentary source; and no ReNew, Tata Power or NTPC NETRA startup programme open to a foreign vendor was found.

## The guerrilla tactics that work are data-led; the illegal ones are drones and cold WhatsApp

Rigorous evidence for B2B "guerrilla" stunts in energy or industrial hardware is close to non-existent, and the few numbers that circulate are agency anecdotes: an out-of-home campaign that reportedly won Aladdin Knowledge Systems meetings with 35% of recipients and Canon's oversized packages to C-suite targets ([Stratabeat, agency roundup, no primary source](https://stratabeat.com/guerrilla-marketing/)); a ~USD 22,000 non-sponsor street campaign around a conference that lifted inbound meeting requests 40% and an invite-only C-suite dinner series in which 67% of 72 attendees agreed to a follow-up within two weeks (page garbled, unverified) ([American Guerrilla Marketing, anecdotes](https://americanguerrillamarketing.com/articles/guerrilla-marketing-case-studies/)). Vendor-reported ABM mailer results are larger but uncontrolled: Zendesk's "15X ROI" and Gainsight's ~USD 5.5 million of influenced pipeline ([Sendoso: Zendesk](https://www.sendoso.com/success-stories/zendesk); [Sendoso: Gainsight](https://www.sendoso.com/success-stories/gainsight)). What the stronger evidence supports is content and proof. The 2024 Edelman–LinkedIn study of ~3,500 decision-makers across seven markets including India found **73% trust thought leadership more than marketing materials**, 86% are more likely to invite producers of high-quality thought leadership into RFPs, 70% of C-suite questioned existing supplier relationships because of it, and only 13% rate what they read as very good ([Edelman report](https://www.edelman.com/expertise/business-marketing/2024-b2b-thought-leadership-report); [full PDF](https://www.edelman.com/sites/g/files/aatuss191/files/2024-02/_2024%20Edelman-LinkedIn%20B2B%20Thought%20Leadership%20Impact%20Report%20Final.pdf)); McKinsey's 2026 B2B Pulse concludes omnichannel engagement "is now the baseline for competing, not a differentiator" ([McKinsey 2026 PDF](https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/mckinsey/business%20functions/marketing%20and%20sales/our%20insights/the%20surprising%20economics%20of%20b2b%20growth%20the%20new%20survival%20threshold%20and%20what%20it%20takes%20to%20thrive/the-surprising-economics-of-b2b-growth-the-new-survival-threshold-and-what-it-takes-to-thrive.pdf)); outreach benchmarks (all vendor or secondary) put cold email at ~1–2% reply, LinkedIn DMs at 10.3% and video prospecting at 8–15% ([Fundraise Insider](https://fundraiseinsider.com/blog/using-video-for-sales-prospecting/); [STACK BD citing Belkins/Expandi](https://stackbd.com/video-prospecting-roi-2026-benchmark-report/)). In this sector the only well-documented early go-to-market, Ecoppia's, was proof-first via one anchor asset owner, not a stunt ([Israel Trade Australia](https://israeltrade.org.au/2014/11/28/this-israeli-startup-makes-robots-that-dry-clean-solar-panels/)). For a self-cleaning vendor, "the stunt" should be the data: a publicly reported, independently measured yield gain on a named plant.

The tactics with the best fit and lowest risk are therefore data-led and credibility-led. A published regional soiling-loss index built from NREL's 255-site fleet map, Solargis's soiling model and satellite methods ([NREL FY24](https://docs.nrel.gov/docs/fy24osti/88769.pdf); [Solargis](https://kb.solargis.com/docs/soiling-losses); [Supe et al., Remote Sensing 2020](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/12/9/1466)) aggregated by region rather than by named plant, with an open ROI calculator, is a tender-list wedge that costs founder time; PVRADAR already markets satellite-based soiling assessment for utility PV, which shows the format is credible ([Terrapinn speaker bio](https://www.terrapinn.com/exhibition/solar-storage-live-zurich/speaker-thore-MÜLLER.stm)). A "free soiling audit" that uses only the owner's SCADA export under NDA plus public data, with no site entry and no drones, speaks the asset manager's language of PR and kWh. Co-authored validation with an institute, following NREL's anti-soiling test plan with Pellucere or the VDI 3956 method ([NREL FY25](https://docs.nrel.gov/docs/fy25osti/96625.pdf); [ScienceDirect, VDI 3956](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0927024819303770)), takes six to twelve months but is what IPP and lender diligence asks for. Hyper-personalised physical mailers to 20–40 named O&M heads at business addresses, hosted dinners for 15–25 asset managers next to a conference, a demo rig in a partner's stand or a hotel suite rather than on a live array, podcast guesting, award entries and trade-press op-eds led by pilot data all sit in the medium-fit, low-risk band, with the caveat that hospitality for PSU and utility staff must stay modest and documented. Risk-reversal pilots ("no measured gain, no fee", or a discounted paid pilot with liquidated-damages-style terms against a weather-adjusted baseline, a structure vendors already describe ([Taypro, vendor](https://taypro.in/blog/performance-guarantee-clauses-in-solar-cleaning-contracts))) work for the first two or three anchors provided baseline, control rows and a go/no-go date are pre-agreed; no documented "no-gain-no-fee" solar cleaning contract was found to calibrate pricing.

The tactics to exclude are specific. Unsanctioned drone imagery of plants: India's Drone Rules 2021 zone airspace through DigitalSky, solar parks "near airstrips, refineries, industrial corridors, or defence facilities frequently enter" permission-required airspace ([Kodainya](https://www.kodainya.com/blogs/drones-for-solar-panel-inspection)), commercial flights need a Remote Pilot Certificate and insurance, and practitioners insist on "written permission from asset owner" before flying over infrastructure ([Elion](https://elion.co.in/legal-and-regulatory-considerations-for-drone-thermography-operations/)); in the US the FAA "does not require property owner consent for overflight" but Part 107, flights over people and state privacy and trespass law still apply ([UAV Coach](https://uavcoach.com/drone-over-private-property/)). Uninvited site entry or "free cleaning": a New York facility security plan requires every visitor to be approved and to attend safety orientation before entry ([Flint Mine Solar security plan](https://documents.dps.ny.gov/public/Common/ViewDoc.aspx?DocRefId=%7BE0FD989E-0000-C436-9EF8-AACFDCFF632A%7D&DocTitle=Appendix+C_Site+Security+Plan)), SolarPower Europe's guidelines require a permit-to-work "before any work starts on-site" with the asset owner holding "ultimate legal and moral responsibility" ([Solar Best Practices, H&S](https://solarbestpractices.com/guidelines/detail/health-and-safety-management)), and cleaning with chemicals, abrasives or pressure breaches module-OEM manuals and exposes the customer's warranty ([Jinko manual](https://www.jinkosolar.com/uploads/JinkoSolar%20Global%20Installation%20Manual_20240814_A1.pdf)). Cold WhatsApp or SMS blasts in India and the Gulf: obtain explicit opt-in before first contact, use the official WhatsApp Business API with approved templates ([Vedain](https://vedain.com/blog/how-to-use-whatsapp-for-b2b-sales-in-india-a-complete-guide); [Rajesh R Nair](https://rajeshrnair.com/blog/business/whatsapp-business/how-to-use-whatsapp-for-b2b-sales-in-indian-and-gcc-markets.html)), and never message before 9 AM or after 8 PM. Scraped personal-data prospect lists for India and the EU fall foul of DPDP and GDPR. The safe substitutes keep the visual hook: satellite or public soiling data instead of drone images; drone or thermal only after signed overflight consent with a certified pilot and airspace check; demos on a vendor-owned rig or a partner test bed; and "cleaning one row" only under the plant's own permit-to-work with the O&M contractor executing or supervising.

A realistic 90-day guerrilla envelope (cost figures are the researcher's estimates, not sourced) is USD 15–40k of cash plus founder time, sequenced from cheapest to dearest: days 1–15, a 50-plant target map from public COD and tender news, the soiling index, the ROI calculator and a two-to-three-times-weekly LinkedIn cadence (USD 0–500); days 10–40, data-only audits, 20–40 personalised ROI one-pagers sent lawfully by region, 60–90-second videos for the top 20 accounts, warranty-compatibility requests to two or three module and tracker OEMs and an institute verification plan (USD 1–3k); days 30–70, one or two risk-reversal pilots on alternating rows under permit-to-work and an 8–12-person practitioner roundtable (USD 3–10k); days 60–90, a hosted dinner and demo suite around Bharat RE Summit, Intersolar India or RE+, a small mailer wave such as a soiling-sample vial from the prospect's own region with the ROI card, and publication of the first pilot dataset with the engineer's sign-off (USD 8–25k). India's consent regime from May 2027 rewards this inbound bias, because the funnel otherwise breaks at the DPDP deadline.

Gaps to verify: no primary-source industrial B2B guerrilla campaign with measured executive meetings exists; no sourced solar example of a lawful PR stunt such as cleaning a landmark with permission; no evidence of plant-manager WhatsApp communities or regional meetups near Bhadla, Khavda or Pavagada; no NISE or IIT certification route for cleaning or anti-soiling technologies; no tracker-OEM partnership examples beyond Nextracker and GameChange; DigitalSky zoning for specific parks must be checked per site; and all cost ranges above are estimates.

## The ranked plan: validation stack, anchor pilot, anchor IPP, then channels and tenders

Geography should follow the product. A module-glass-integrated or factory-applied self-cleaning surface sells at design stage to module OEMs and EPCs, where robots are already specified in Gulf tenders and GW-scale Indian orders, so the right first conversations are Covestro-style OEM integration, tracker OEMs and the EPCs writing the specification. A retrofit coating, EDS overlay or new mechanical system sells to owners of fleets built before about 2020 and should start where soiling is high but robot penetration and labour costs make a non-robot approach attractive, the US Southwest, the Atacama and Australia's interior, while treating India and the Gulf as markets to enter through a partner whose robot fleet the product complements rather than displaces. In every case the pitch is written in the owner's KPIs, delta PR and soiling ratio, OPEX per MW, litres per MW per year and the P50 and DSCR uplift, and it is backed by the four bankability artefacts before the first executive meeting is requested.

## Conclusion

The research reframes the salesperson's job. Access to solar executives is not the scarce resource: their names sit in BIS committee rosters, Solarplaza's attendee database, Mercom's deal coverage and conference programmes, and they gather predictably in San Diego, Prague, Greater Noida, Gandhinagar and Abu Dhabi. What is scarce is permission to be taken seriously, and in this category that permission is granted by a module maker's warranty letter, an abrasion test on the buyer's own glass, a measured control-row result and an independent engineer who will let the soiling line in the financial model move. The pioneers that skipped that discipline and chased scale through public listings (Ecoppia, BladeRanger) were overtaken within four years by cheaper Chinese hardware once buyers had been de-risked; the entrants still standing either own a channel (Taypro's O&M model, Nextracker's bundling) or a reference that a national champion wanted to showcase (NOMADD at NEOM, Ecoppia at Ketura and Bhadla).

Two implications follow for the next eighteen months. First, the most valuable sale is not to an O&M contractor but to the owner's asset manager and, through the independent engineer, to the lender, because a documented one-point cut in the soiling assumption on a 100 MW plant is worth more than any cleaning invoice; that argues for shipping the technology with soiling sensors and publishing the first pilot as a methods paper rather than a press release. Second, India's May 2027 consent deadline and the Gulf's opt-in rules will shut the cold-outreach funnel that Western playbooks assume, so the inbound assets, the soiling index, the audits and the institute-backed data, are not marketing extras but the only durable lead source in the two markets where soiling matters most. The gaps flagged throughout, above all current robot unit prices, named O&M heads at the Indian majors and any IE's formal treatment of self-cleaning technology, are the first things a salesperson should close in person, because they are exactly the questions the first anchor customer will ask.
